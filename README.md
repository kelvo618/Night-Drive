# Night-Drive
A 3D night-driving game that runs in the browser. Play it in landscape on a phone, or on a computer with a keyboard.
Play it: https://Kelvo618.github.io/night-drive
Game modes
Taxi shift (3 minutes)
Pick up passengers and drop them off to earn fares.
Brake to a near stop inside the glowing green bay to pick someone up.
Stop in the orange bay ahead to drop them off.
A crash costs $20 and removes your tip. Missing a stop costs $10.
Gangster run
Grab loot, move through different streets and stay ahead of the police.
Collect green cash bags for $50 each.
Every 900 m you enter a new street (Downtown, Docklands, Chinatown, Old Town) with its own look and a $150 bonus.
The police get faster and more numerous the longer you survive. If one catches you, you're busted.
